On Earth Day weekend, 134 volunteers worked six sites along the Sallow.
By one o'clock the dumpster at the boat ramp was full, and we had
counted everything that went into it.

- 41 tires, most of them old enough to have no tread pattern left
- 1,906 plastic bottles
- 2 shopping carts
- 1 car battery
- 1 upright piano, missing its keys, wedged under the railroad bridge

> "Nobody can tell us how the piano got there. It took eight people and a winch."

## Smaller is better

In 2009 our first cleanup filled four dumpsters. This one filled one.
That is not because people stopped caring; it is because most of the old
dumps along the banks are gone, and the township now fines anyone caught
dumping tires.

What is left is mostly plastic that washes off the roads after storms.
That will not end with cleanups alone, so this year we are asking the
township for storm-drain screens on Mill Street.

## Next time

The fall cleanup is on the last Saturday in October. Bring boots that can
get wet.
