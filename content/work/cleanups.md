The first cleanup in 2009 filled four dumpsters with tires, appliances
and a car door. These days a spring cleanup fills one, and most of it is
plastic bottles washed down from the roads after storms.

We provide gloves, grabbers, trash bags, waders for the deep spots and
coffee. The township hauls the dumpsters away for free. Tires go to a
recycler in Zanesville.
