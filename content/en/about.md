title: Started by anglers and a science teacher, run by its members
lead: In 2009, a handful of people who fished the Sallow got tired of watching it fill up with tires and silt. They met in the back of the Wren Hollow library, and Gene Albright, who taught science at the high school, brought his class's water-test results.
---

The first cleanup that spring filled four dumpsters. The first trees went
in two years later, on a farm whose owner had seen the cleanup in the
paper. Since then the alliance has grown to 1,400 members, three staff and
hundreds of volunteers a year, and it still works the same way: one
stretch of river at a time, with the people who own the land along it.

We are a member organization. Members elect the board at the annual
meeting every March, and anyone who has paid dues can stand for it.
