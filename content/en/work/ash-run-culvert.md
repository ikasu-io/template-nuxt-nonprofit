Ash Run flows under County Road 9 through a corrugated steel pipe from the
1960s. At low water it drops out of the pipe onto rocks a foot and a half
below; no fish can jump that. In floods the pipe is too small and the
road goes under water.

The county wants to replace it anyway. We are working with the county
engineer so that the new crossing is a short bridge, wide enough for the
creek to keep its natural bed. The design work starts in 2027, and the
grant applications are going out this winter.
